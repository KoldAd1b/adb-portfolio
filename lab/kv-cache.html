Model Selection and KV-Cache Allocation Are Coupled under Memory Constraints

A. Adib, S. Ahmed, L. Khan

NeurIPS 2026 LCFM Workshop.

NeurIPS page

Summary

On a device with fixed memory, weights and KV cache must fit in one budget, and a smaller cache lowers quality. We tested many models at many context lengths, and the top-scoring model changed as context grew. Choosing a model first and trimming its cache afterward gave lower scores than choosing both together.
