What Do Expert Interventions Measure in Mixture-of-Experts Models?

S. Ahmed, A. Adib, P. K. Saha, L. Khan

NeurIPS 2026 Interpretability as a Science Workshop.

Summary

Researchers switch off or rescale whole experts to explain a prediction from a mixture-of-experts model. We compared that with editing a few neurons selected by their direct effect on the predicted token. Removing those neurons lowered the target's probability more than removing whole experts, which could raise it. Amplifying them raised low-probability targets and had little or negative effect on targets that already had high probability.
