The Unseen-Mode Problem: Certifying Coverage Failures in Molecular Generators

A. Adib, S. Ahmed, L. Khan

NeurIPS 2026 SIMBIOCHEM Workshop.

Summary

A Boltzmann generator can score well on overlap with a reference and still have almost no samples in a state with a large share of the population. We built an exact lower confidence bound on the probability missing from a basin against a fixed reference, and we found these gaps in released generators of several kinds. We also proved that target mass in a basin cannot be detected from samples if no samples fall in that basin.
